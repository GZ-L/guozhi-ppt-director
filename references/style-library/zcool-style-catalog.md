## Selection Defaults

- Formal business or report: prefer full packs `ref-02`, `ref-05`, `ref-20`, or `ref-23`.
- Technology launch: prefer full packs `ref-01`, `ref-04`, `ref-05`, or `ref-16`.
- AI workflow or software product: prefer full packs `ref-04`, `ref-16`, or `ref-20`.
- Consumer campaign: no current full pack is a deterministic default; request a new reference unless the user accepts a cover-only entry.
- Product/hardware keynote: prefer full packs `ref-01`, `ref-02`, `ref-05`, or `ref-23`; use `ref-07` only after explicit limited-coverage acceptance.
- Automotive or mobility strategy: prefer `ref-02`, `ref-05`, or `ref-23`.
- Playful IP or creator deck: request a new full reference; legacy `ref-06`, `ref-08`, `ref-14`, and `ref-15` are cover-only.
- Warm lifestyle or invitation deck: prefer full pack `ref-18`; legacy `ref-09` is cover-only.
- High-drama dark pitch: prefer `ref-19` or `ref-20`.
- Elegant editorial/portfolio deck: prefer full pack `ref-18`.
- Process-heavy workflow explainer: prefer full packs `ref-04`, `ref-16`, `ref-20`, or `ref-23`.

When a style pack exists for a reference, prefer the pack over the single catalog image. Use the catalog row to shortlist mood and topic fit, then use `style-pack-index.json` to check page-role coverage before production.

## Catalog Scoring Reference

Use the eight-dimension scoring anchors and tie-break order in `style-match-rules.md`. This catalog supplies candidate metadata; it does not override eligibility, score, or route constraints.

## Content Constraints

- For AI article decks, do not default to dark sci-fi. Use it only if the content is launch-like; otherwise prefer `ref-10`, `ref-11`, or `ref-13` and add contrast through typography, diagrams, and spacing.
- For business reports, keep body pages calmer than the cover reference. Avoid poster-style giant type on every page.
- For dense methodology content, prefer modular grids, diagrams, and clean callouts over cinematic imagery.
- For source-heavy decks, make reference choice support legibility first: screenshots and quotations need stable frames, not decorative overlays.
- If the top-scoring reference is below the minimum score, request a new reference instead of applying a subjective override.
