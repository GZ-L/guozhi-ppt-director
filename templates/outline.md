## Notes

- Match the exact confirmed page count; do not render from an unresolved range.
- Give each page one job only.
- Split any page that contains two unrelated messages.
- Keep slide text short; put detailed explanation in `Speaker Note`.
- Fill `Layout Contract` before rendering. Include the selected style-pack role, fixed skeleton, media slots, diagram/data slots, and forbidden changes.
- Fill `Visible Text Budget` with title/body/list/code limits. Rewrite overlong text before changing the visual style.
- For motion decks, record the in-slide choreography job, three to five semantic beats per content slide, grouped builds, semantic reveal order, transition, stable object names plus final shape/scene IDs, triggers, effects, durations, and video media-slot IDs. Dynamic card/image requests use more than one visible entrance family unless target compatibility or reduced motion requires all-fade. Use automatic sequencing inside an explanation and clicks only at semantic checkpoints. Transition-only is insufficient unless explicitly confirmed. Renderers may not invent extra animation.
- When richer visuals are requested, fill two to four content-bearing media slots across a short deck. Prefer authorized source visuals, keep them readable and attributed, and pair every image with editable captions or conclusions rather than generic decoration.
